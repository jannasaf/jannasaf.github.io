Hello!

My philosophy is simple: people first, build second, iterate frequently.

I'm an AI Conversation Designer working at the intersection of healthcare, human behavior, communication, systems, and AI. I'm currently at Anonymous Health, a behavioral health technology startup, where I design the conversations and decision logic behind our AI systems.

My undergraduate sociology work looked at how the language around health and disease shapes stigma, self-perception, and whether people seek care. My MPH at Columbia went deeper into population health, health systems strengthening, behavioral science, and health communications, and into how people access and engage with care in the first place.

Since then, the work has followed the same pattern regardless of title: research how people actually behave, find the friction and the opportunity, translate that into a change to a system or a conversation, watch what happens, then iterate. AI conversation design is not a departure from that work. It's a new medium for it.
